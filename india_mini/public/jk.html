Culture of Jammu & Kashmir

Jammu & Kashmir has a rich cultural heritage influenced by Hindu, Buddhist and Islamic traditions. Traditional Kashmiri dress includes Pheran for both men and women. Music, handicrafts like Pashmina shawls and carpet weaving are very famous. Festivals like Eid, Lohri and Baisakhi are celebrated with great joy. The region is also known for its hospitality and peaceful lifestyle.
